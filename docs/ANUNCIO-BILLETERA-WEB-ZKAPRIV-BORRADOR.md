## English

**Zoka Quantic Testnet v2 · Web wallet and ZKAPriv 3.0.0**

• Tor stays optional.
• Installs over 2.1.x: restore your Quantic wallet with its 24 words.

Tested on Nov 4 with a full cycle: ZSilent → web wallet → ZKAPriv → ZSilent, mined in blocks 4,789, 4,791 and 4,809.
