This board tracks execution status for the launch-critical stream.

| Workstream | Owner | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

- Monday: open blockers and ownership updates.
- Wednesday: gate review and risk escalation.
- Friday: go/no-go checkpoint.
